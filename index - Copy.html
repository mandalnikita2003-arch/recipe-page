The evening CHAI

This evening chai recipe is straight out from my mom's kitchen where making chai was never just a routine but a comforting therapy ,filling the entire room with the aroma of adrak chai .

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup of water
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 2 teaspoons of chai patti
  • 1 inch of ginger (adrak)
  • 2 Elaichi
  • 2 spoons of sugar

Instructions

  1. Take 1.5 cup of water and keep it for a good boil and then add crushed ginger and elaichi
  2. After wait for 2-3 minute and add two teaspoons of chaipatti
  3. Pour in 1 cup of milk and sugar according to what you prefer
  4. Lower the heat and let it simmer until it turns into a rich caramel color
  5. Strain the hot chai through a tea strainer into your fav evening cup and enjoy
